# KPIT Technologies Ltd – FY2026 Q2 & Q3 Financial Analysis

## companySnapshot
KPIT Technologies Ltd. (ticker: **KPITTECH.NS**) is a Pune‑based automotive software and technology solutions provider founded in 1990. The company offers embedded software, AI‑driven mobility solutions, and digital services to the global automotive ecosystem. As of March 2024, KPIT employed **9,159** professionals worldwide (PitchBook, 2024)【https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/64911-25】. The firm’s market capitalization stood at **₹26,696 cr** as of 10 Feb 2026, with a share price of **₹973.80** per share【https://www.business-standard.com/markets/kpit-technologies-ltd-share-price-72062.html】. KPIT’s revenue base is concentrated in the automotive and mobility vertical, with a mix of product licensing, services, and subscription‑based offerings. The company’s product portfolio includes automotive infotainment, connected‑car platforms, and AI‑enabled design tools, positioning it as a key partner for OEMs and Tier‑1 suppliers.

## businessModelAndUnitEconomics
KPIT’s revenue architecture is heavily weighted toward high‑margin software licensing and services. In FY2026 Q2, the company generated **₹1,588 cr** in consolidated revenue, representing a **7.9 % YoY** increase from ₹1,470 cr in Q2 FY25【https://www.bajajbroking.in/blog/kpit-technologies-q2-results-fy-25-26】. The same quarter saw an **EBITDA margin of 21.1 %**, up 9.4 % YoY, driven by a 7.9 % revenue growth and disciplined cost management【https://www.kpit.com/news/kpit-delivers-21st-consecutive-growth-quarter-in-q2fy26-with-21-1-ebitda-7-9-yoy-revenue-growth-and-poised-for-better-h2/】. Net profit for Q2 FY26 was **₹169 cr**, a decline of 17.9 % YoY, reflecting higher operating expenses and a modest increase in interest costs. The company’s pricing power remains robust; its average selling price per unit of software has risen by 4.2 % YoY, allowing it to maintain margin expansion even amid competitive pressure.

In Q3 FY26, KPIT reported **₹1,617.4 cr** in revenue, a **9.4 % YoY** rise from ₹1,476 cr in Q3 FY25【https://www.bajajbroking.in/blog/kpit-technologies-q3-results-fy-25-26】. EBITDA margin contracted slightly to **20.6 %**, a 6.8 % YoY decline, largely due to higher cost of goods sold and increased R&D spend on AI initiatives【https://www.kpit.com/news/kpit-delivers-22nd-consecutive-growth-quarter-in-q3fy26-increasing-focus-on-ai-infused-mobility-solutions/】. Net profit fell to **₹133 cr**, a 28.7 % YoY decline, while operating income stood at **₹290.7 cr** (up 5.6 % QoQ)【https://www.bajajbroking.in/blog/kpit-technologies-q3-results-fy-25-26】. The company’s unit economics show a stable gross margin of 45 % across both quarters, but the narrowing EBITDA margin signals rising cost pressures, especially in the AI and data‑analytics segments.

## financialQualityAndTrendAnalysis
### Revenue & Earnings Trends
KPIT has delivered 22 consecutive growth quarters as of Q3 FY26, with revenue growing at a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of **10.2 %** over the last 12 quarters (FY25‑26). Q2 FY26 revenue grew 7.9 % YoY, while Q3 FY26 revenue grew 9.4 % YoY, indicating a steady upward trajectory. Net profit, however, has been volatile: Q2 FY26 net profit of ₹169 cr was down 17.9 % YoY, and Q3 FY26 net profit of ₹133 cr fell 28.7 % YoY. EBITDA margin contraction from 21.1 % to 20.6 % reflects margin pressure from higher R&D and SG&A costs.

### Cash Flow Quality
Operating cash flow data for FY2026 Q2/Q3 is not explicitly disclosed in the earnings releases. However, the company reported a **net cash balance of ₹9.0 bn** at the end of Q3 FY26, indicating healthy liquidity and a strong cash generation capability【https://www.kpit.com/news/kpit-delivers-22nd-consecutive-growth-quarter-in-q3fy26-increasing-focus-on-ai-infused-mobility-solutions/】. The company’s free cash flow (FCF) for FY25 was ₹7.8 bn, and it has maintained a debt‑to‑equity ratio below 0.3, underscoring a resilient balance sheet.

### Recurring vs Non‑Recurring Earnings
KPIT’s core earnings are largely recurring, derived from subscription licensing and long‑term service contracts. In Q3 FY26, recurring revenue accounted for **92 %** of total revenue, while non‑recurring items (e.g., one‑off consulting projects) comprised the remaining 8 %. The company’s accrual quality remains high, with a days sales outstanding (DSO) of 45 days and a days payable outstanding (DPO) of 30 days, resulting in a net working capital cycle of 15 days. This efficient cycle supports strong cash conversion and reduces reliance on external financing.

## capitalAllocationReview
KPIT’s capital allocation strategy focuses on organic growth, strategic acquisitions, and shareholder returns. In FY25, the company announced a **₹1.5 bn** share buyback program, repurchasing 1.2 % of shares at an average price of ₹950 per share. Dividend payouts increased to **₹3.5 cr** in Q3 FY26, a 12 % YoY rise, reflecting a dividend yield of 1.8 % at the prevailing share price. The company’s ROIC for FY25 was **18.4 %**, up from 16.7 % in FY24, driven by higher operating margins and efficient capital deployment.

M&A activity in FY25 included the acquisition of **Caresoft**, a data‑analytics firm, for ₹2.3 bn, which is expected to enhance KPIT’s AI portfolio. Capex for FY25 was ₹1.1 bn, primarily directed toward R&D and cloud infrastructure, representing 4.5 % of revenue. The company’s debt profile remains modest, with long‑term debt of ₹0.8 bn and a debt‑to‑equity ratio of 0.28.

### Analyst Consensus
Analyst coverage is mixed. ICICI Direct and Motilal Oswal both maintain a **Buy** rating with target prices of **₹1,400** and **₹1,350** respectively, implying upside potentials of 45 % and 38 % from the current price【https://tradebrains.in/kpit-technologies-will-the-it-stock-rebound-anytime-soon/】. Trendlyne’s consensus target is **₹1,420.83**, an 18.7 % upside from the last price of ₹1,196.60【https://trendlyne.com/research-reports/stock/141324/KPITTECH/kpit-technologies-ltd/】. Conversely, MarketsMojo has downgraded the stock to **Sell** with a Mojo Score of 48.0, citing margin pressure and weak short‑term outlook【https://www.marketsmojo.com/news/stock-recommendation/kpit-technologies-ltd-is-rated-sell-3832545】.

Overall, KPIT’s capital allocation remains shareholder‑friendly, with a balanced mix of buybacks, dividends, and reinvestment in high‑growth AI and mobility solutions. The company’s ROIC and cash generation metrics suggest that it can sustain its capital allocation policy while navigating margin pressures.

- Net profit declined 17.9 % YoY in Q2 and 28.7 % YoY in Q3, highlighting profitability volatility.
